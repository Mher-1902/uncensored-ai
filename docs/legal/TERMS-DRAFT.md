## The service

Offscript provides AI-generated responses through an account-based website. Fast replies directly; Deep Think uses a larger thinking budget. Neither currently searches the web. Model behavior, answer quality and availability can vary. We do not guarantee that every request will produce an answer.

Chats are temporary. Clearing, reloading or leaving the chat page removes its conversation from that page. There is no chat history to restore. Review the Privacy Policy for account information, processing by infrastructure providers, and the limits of this temporary-chat design.
